Common Questions

MEMBERSHIP FAQS

Is there a registration signup fee?

No, we do not believe in administrative signup hidden charges. Your selected subscription pricing is exactly what you pay to activate your card.

Can I pause my subscription plans?

Yes. Elite members can pause memberships once per year for up to 30 days. VIP members enjoy unlimited account freezes up to 90 days annually.

How do I book 1-on-1 personal coaches?

Once you sign up for the Elite or VIP tiers, you can pair up with a coach via our reception counter or by emailing booking@titanfitnessgym.com.

Do you offer trial access passes?

Yes! We offer a one-day all-access trial pass for local residents. You can register for a trial on our Contact Page or by visiting our front desk.
